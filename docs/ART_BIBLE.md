# WORD QUEST RUN — ART BIBLE

## Current character palette lock

Lumi's active gameplay palette is:
- hair: silver / soft lilac
- costume: deep midnight navy
- trim: antique warm gold
- magic/crystal: luminous violet

This is the active direction for `visual-master-v1`. Avoid reverting to the earlier blonde + bright-purple prototype. Character age impression remains approximately 16–18, with a teenage anime-fantasy heroine silhouette rather than chibi proportions.

## Visual principle
WORD QUEST RUN is visual-first: the player should feel overwhelmed by scale, light, depth and motion while answering English through three in-world paths. Background spectacle, character appeal, readable choices and kinetic motion take priority over dense lore.
